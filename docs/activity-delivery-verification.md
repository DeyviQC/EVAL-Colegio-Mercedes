Date: 2026-10-08 (America/Lima). Human approval: Continue after the concrete proposal for informational dates, text/file evidence up to 10 MiB and retained updates. The collaborator's professor-approved Activities and Deliveries screens remain the visual baseline.

## Real application behavior

Teacher/student navigation now contains Activities and Deliveries, with an authorized course/aula selector and bounded pagination. Teachers publish a required title and instructions with an optional school-local due date, and explicitly close activities. The due date does not automatically deny deliveries. Students submit nonblank text, one PDF/JPG/PNG/TXT/DOCX file, or both; actual server file/content validation and the configurable initial 10 MiB limit apply. Initial acceptance preserves original assignment and active enrollment automatically.

A student updates the same logical delivery only while the activity and parent period are open and their active enrollment still equals the original accepted-under enrollment. Each confirmed update replaces current answer/evidence and retains the previous immutable version. Text-only updates do not erase older files. The original teacher consults received work; the student sees only their own deliveries. Current and historical version downloads enforce ownership on the server. Successor teachers and management roles receive no delivery browsing rights. Assignment closure/replacement alone does not redirect otherwise eligible work. Consulting deliveries refreshes activity state and action flags.

Three additive tables preserve the immutable prerequisite references and their triggers. Writers share the guard and canonical lock protocol; publication or acceptance is acknowledged only after reference/content/version/evidence commit. Confirmed rejected writes clean only their own unreferenced staged file; uncertain commits retain potentially referenced files and are never automatically replayed. Evidence files live in ignored .local/eval-dev/deliveries, separate from material storage. No older prerequisite database was rebuilt or granted these new permissions.
