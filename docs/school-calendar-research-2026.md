Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: Peru's national 2026 basic education guidance, not verification of the school's approved calendar.

## Official findings

MINEDU RM 501-2025, sections 5.2.1–5.2.2, provides 36 teaching weeks and eight management weeks. Its suggested schedule is:

| Type | Weeks | Dates in 2026 |
|---|---:|---|
| Management | 2 | March 2–13 |
| Teaching | 9 | March 16–May 15 |
| Management | 1 | May 18–22 |
| Teaching | 9 | May 25–July 24 |
| Management | 2 | July 27–August 7 |
| Teaching | 9 | August 10–October 9 |
| Management | 1 | October 12–16 |
| Teaching | 9 | October 19–December 18 |
| Management | 2 | December 21–31 |

Management time supports staff planning, monitoring and improvement. Calendar organization may use bimesters, trimesters or another structure. DRE/UGEL contextual adjustments are possible; the institution records its calendar in its PAT or applicable management document and communicates it to the community and UGEL. The 160 teaching/30 management day minimum is an emergency reference; ordinary institutions must exceed those minima. Four teaching blocks do not establish a compulsory four-bimester model for every institution.

Sources: [MINEDU 2026 technical standard](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/11753), [official PDF](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/11753/Norma%20T%C3%A9cnica%20para%20el%20A%C3%B1o%20Escolar%202026%20en%20las%20Instituciones%20y%20Programas%20Educativos%20P%C3%BAblicos%20y%20Privados%20de%20la%20Educaci%C3%B3n%20B%C3%A1sica.pdf?sequence=1), [management weeks guidance](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/11636). ## EVAL design recommendation (not approved behavior)

- Use a school-wide calendar per academic year, confirmed by management from the approved PAT and applicable DRE/UGEL guidance. Do not let each teacher independently redefine institutional week dates.
- Derive student week groups from teaching weeks; distinguish management blocks instead of presenting them as ordinary teaching weeks.
- Allow the owning teacher to associate authorized materials/activities with a school teaching week, with Unassigned retained for general resources. Editing authority and reassignment rules require review.
- Show dates beside week numbers and the institution's actual bimester/trimester label when applicable. Continuous teaching-week numbering is a proposal, not a national mandate.
- Preserve activity deadlines, explicit closure, permissions, original ownership, routing and historical context independently of calendar grouping.
- Treat 2026 dates as an editable reference for review, not permanent constants or the verified school calendar. Future years need their own applicable guidance.
