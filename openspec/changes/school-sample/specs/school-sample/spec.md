# Local Fictional School Sample

The development-only sample SHALL use existing authorized commands and actor scopes. It SHALL retain original data and credentials, use grades 1–5 with sections A–H, and clearly identify generated people/learning resources as fictional. It SHALL NOT claim a verified institutional roster or timetable.

Repeated execution in the same active period SHALL reuse its existing accounts, scopes and named resources rather than duplicate deliveries or assessments. An identity/scope collision SHALL fail rather than overwrite an existing person or original assignment. The normal existing student's enrollment SHALL remain unchanged.

Generated PDFs SHALL contain original readable content. Actual student course/material/grade reads SHALL preserve grade/section and ownership boundaries. The command SHALL remain bound to the owned loopback development database, omit credentials from output, and add no public seed endpoint or remote mutation.

DNI/PIN policy SHALL remain separate from fictional data loading. No real DNI import, passwordless login or shorter-secret rule is approved by this sample request.
