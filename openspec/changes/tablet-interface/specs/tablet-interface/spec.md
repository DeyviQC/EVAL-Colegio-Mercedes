# Tablet Interface Requirements

EVAL SHALL preserve its actual role-authorized functionality while adapting existing navigation and content to desktop, tablet portrait/landscape and narrow viewports. The active section SHALL be visible, and narrow navigation SHALL retain every authorized destination behind an explicitly labeled section control.

Primary buttons, navigation, action links and material option summaries SHALL have at least 44 CSS pixels of height. Input text SHALL remain readable, keyboard focus visible and main content directly reachable by a skip link. These selected goals SHALL NOT be represented as complete accessibility certification.

Material maintenance SHALL be mounted and its initial status fetched only after the teacher explicitly opens that material's options. Unknown outcome barriers SHALL remain in the shared client across closing/reopening, and reopening SHALL NOT automatically repeat a write.

Motion SHALL be brief and finite, limited to transform/opacity, and disabled for reduced motion. The redesign SHALL add no runtime external asset dependency, continuous animation or new UI/animation package. Device-emulated checks SHALL be distinguished from real institutional tablet acceptance.
